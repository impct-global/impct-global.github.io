Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31/08/2026

IMPCT ("we", "the Site") respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle (or rather, don't handle) your data when you visit this website.

*No Data Collection

This website does not collect, store, or process any personal data from its users. We do not use cookies, analytics tools, tracking pixels, or any similar technologies.

No Cookies

This site does not install technical, profiling, or third-party cookies. As a result, no cookie consent banner is required, since no processing that would require one takes place.

Hosting and Technical Server Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically record standard technical logs (e.g. IP address, user agent) for security and service operation purposes. This is not under our direct control and is not accessed by us for profiling purposes. For more details, see GitHub's Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Links to Third-Party Sites

This site may contain links to external websites (e.g. social media, external forms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of such third-party sites; please review their respective privacy policies.

Contact Forms / Email

If you voluntarily contact us via email or a contact form, the data you provide (e.g. name, email) is used solely to respond to you and is not shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy in the future, for example if we later introduce analytics or cookies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
