Why impct exists

I'm Mia, founder of impct.

For years, I designed spaces where people come together. But it was inside festivals that I witnessed something extraordinary: thousands of strangers becoming a community.

People from different countries, cultures, and backgrounds connected by music, joy, and a shared experience. It made me realize the power of people coming together around something they love.

I started asking a simple question:

What if that same community could create opportunities for young people who need them most — without changing a single thing about the festival experience?

I couldn't find the infrastructure to make it possible.
So together with Toni, our CTO, we decided to build it.

At impct, we believe that small actions, multiplied by millions of people, can create meaningful change. Our mission is to transform moments of connection into lasting impact and create opportunities for the next generation.
